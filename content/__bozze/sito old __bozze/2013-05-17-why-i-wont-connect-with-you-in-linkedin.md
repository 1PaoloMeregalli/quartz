# Today I have 16 people that want to connect with me in LinkedIN ...

[![Screen Shot 2013-05-04 at 08.51.03](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/Screen-Shot-2013-05-04-at-08.51.03.png)](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/Screen-Shot-2013-05-04-at-08.51.03.png)

# and I will not connect with (a lot of) them !

 Yes, yes, yes I'm not crazy, just I want use linkedIN as a <span style="color: #800000;">**business social network**</span>. I don't want wake up in the morning and write in LinkedIN "ehi good morning guys" only because someone (ie FaceBook) ask to me ### "what's on your mind?"

## I don't want 1500 contacts in LinkedIN that I really don't know!

 My contacts in LinkedIN are people that <span style="color: #800000;">***<span style="text-decoration: underline;">I really know</span>***</span> or that I meet in business project ideas. I don't want give my LinkedIN connection to people that I had never meet or that I don't know. # So I suggest to you don't send to me a LinkedIN invitation if you dont know me !

 If you want to contact me (and you don't know me) you have other channels ... and you can find it in my public LinkedIN profile: professional mail, twitter account, skype name ... it is not difficult to contact me ... isn't it? :-) [![Screen Shot 2013-05-04 at 09.14.53](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/Screen-Shot-2013-05-04-at-09.14.53-300x216.png)](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/Screen-Shot-2013-05-04-at-09.14.53.png)

[![LinkedInOpenlinkNetwork](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/LinkedInOpenlinkNetwork-150x150.jpg)](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/LinkedInOpenlinkNetwork.jpg)Furthermore I have a LinkedIN "Business Account Type" so you can use ["OpenLink"](http://help.linkedin.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/142) network[![get intorduced](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/get-intorduced-150x150.png)](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/get-intorduced.png) or ask to some of our "common" contact to introduce you ([Get Introduced](http://help.linkedin.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1959).)

### Usually I send a "I don't know" message to LinkedIN invitation if "I don't know" ... complex deductive reasoning required to me to take this decision :-)

 ... but this is a problem for you! First: in the future you can not re-invite me another time Second: if you receive an excessive number of "I don't know this person" responses, you could be restricted from sending invitations to connect to others ! (see help linkedin for ["I don't know"](http://help.linkedin.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/2974) and also for the ["LinkedIN restriction"](http://help.linkedin.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1386) ) I don't know exactly what are happening in LinkedIN, but it's a few days that my un-know people invitations are growing too fast. I don't know if they are really interested in something about me and my businesses or only if "LinkedIN Community" decided to take a strong marketing approach :-). ### [![reply](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/reply.png)](http://www.paolomeregalli.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/reply.png)Today I will change my way to answer and I will simply "Reply" to these 16 people!

 ***<span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span style="color: #800000; text-decoration: underline;">I will send to them this post so they will understand better why I have not accepted their LinkedIN invitation.</span></span>*** "Reply" don't signal you as a "spammer" to linkedIN ... so you will have ***<span style="color: #800000;">NO restriction</span>*** from them. Anyway don't get angry with me about the reason I haven't accepted your invitation if I don't know you ... simply use other way to contact me next first time ... ([my public LinkedIN profile has all the information :-)](http://www.linkedin.com/in/paolomeregalli) ) # Give it time !

 PS: If you have same problem feel free to send this post to your un-know people that sent to you a LinkedIN invitation ... ### give them a second chance before click "I don't know" :-)

 ... maybe they don't know all these things about LinkedIN and they could read it through this post and through the links to the LinkedIN help services that I have attached.
